# How the game works (for explaining it to the user)

From the game's own tooltips where marked "tooltip"; the rest is general knowledge of Realm Grinder, not checked in the files.

## Loop
1. Buildings produce coins per second ("74/s" is per second); clicking the realm adds a little.
2. Upgrades multiply production. Costs use suffixes: M, B, T, Qa, Qi, Sx, Sp, Oc, No.
3. "Proof of Good Deed" or "Proof of Evil Deed" (25000 coins) picks the alignment for this run. The user chose Good on 2026-10-11; that unlocked more buildings (Warrior Barracks, Knights Joust, Wizard Tower, Cathedral, Citadel, Royal Castle, Heaven's Gate, Hall of Legends).
4. Then a faction: Fairy, Elven, Angel (Good) or Goblin, Undead, Demon (Evil), joined by spending faction coins, which drop at random.
5. Spells use mana, which refills. Tax Collection (200 mana): a lump of coins. Call to Arms (400 mana, game hotkey 2): raises all building production for 20 seconds (tooltip).
6. The user bought most first-run upgrades by 2026-10-15 and asked if that is the end: no, the loop is grow, join a faction, buy its upgrades, abdicate for gems, repeat; later Reincarnation, Research, Archeology, Ascension.
7. Abdication (top of Upgrades) resets the run for gems; each gem raises production for good. Reincarnation (shown as ??? early) is a deeper reset much later.
8. Trophies are achievements. Rubies are the premium currency. The game produces while closed (Offline Production box at start).

## Details from tooltips
- Assistants gather coins and faction coins passively, also offline: each gives 5% of the clicking reward per second. More come from the first two tiers of building upgrades and later unique upgrades.
- Buying: Control click buys 10, Shift click 100, Control Shift click MAX. The Buy amount button offers 1, 10, 100, 1000, 10000, SMART, MAX.
- Clicks (explained to the user 2026-10-14): a click = pressing the realm ("Click to earn coins" under the Realm heading). Each gives the Clicking Reward in coins and a chance at a Faction Coin ("Faction Coin find chance"). Autoclicks are clicks the game makes on a timer and count the same; Options "Disable Non-Manual Clicks" switches them off. Elven Mint (50 M): doubles FC find chance, autoclicks 3 times a second.
- Factions add no buildings: buildings come from the alignment only. A faction adds its spell (Elves: Moon Blessing, 700 mana; the user has it, so they joined the Elves) and its upgrades. Elves are about clicks and Faction Coins: "Elven Luck" triggers on clicks (also autoclicks) for Faction Coins (game strings).
- Royal Market (free upgrade): Royal Exchanges bought with Faction Coins, +10% production each, price rises per faction (tooltip).
- No time-to-afford or mana-full counter anywhere in the game; the only progress bars are spell time left (also written as "16s") and the mana orb fill.
- The game has no option to shorten tooltips (only "Enable Longtap Tooltips", "Enable Spell Tooltip Persist").
- Scrying is not available on this platform (mobile only, or with the Gift of the Faithful shop upgrade).
